Military ground-control software for operating many unmanned vehicles at once.
As a military project, its features and concept of operations are not published.

Scope: WPF UI for multi-vehicle status monitoring, integration with an in-house
communication protocol (submodule), map/video display, alarm handling.

Stack: WPF · C# · .NET 8
